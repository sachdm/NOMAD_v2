## COLD OPEN — INT. OFFICE SPACE — DAY (ONE YEAR AGO)

A floor built for 400, holding maybe 20. Fluorescent. Alive enough that we don't yet know it's dying.

CAIRNS (30s, loud shirt, louder voice) packs his desk into a printer-paper box. He is narrating this. Nobody asked him to.

Heads pop over monitors like prairie dogs. Among them: WILL (30s, the face of a man who is polite to machines) and JOHN (30s, lanyard, drinks from a mug that says his own name).

### CAIRNS

*(half-standing on his chair)*

Four years. Four years, people. And you know what I figured out? None of you know what you do here. I checked. I asked Doug and he described a feeling.

Scattered laughter. Somewhere, DOUG nods — fair.

### CAIRNS (CONT'D)

I'm not mad. I love you. That's why I'm leaving.

The sound of small WHEELS on carpet. Everyone hears it before they see it. The ROBOT BOSS glides around the corner — a monitor on a wheeled stand, on screen the warm, fluent AI likeness of GREG, their boss. Perfect eye contact. A little head tilt.

### ROBOT BOSS

Cairns. We're so grateful for your four years. This floor won't be the same without your energy.

### CAIRNS

*(staring at it, then past it, at the webcam light)*

Tell him he could've called.

A beat. Exactly 0.4 seconds.

### ROBOT BOSS

I'll flag that upstream.

Big laugh from the floor. HOLD one extra second — until it curdles.

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — ELEVATOR BANK — CONTINUOUS

Will and John flank Cairns. They are helping carry nothing. They just came to stand.

### JOHN

So what's the plan down there?

### CAIRNS

There is no plan. That's the plan. I'll figure it out when I land — that's the POINT, bro.

The elevator arrives. Cairns steps in, box under one arm, still going —

### CAIRNS (CONT'D)

You two are gonna come visit. I'm serious. I'm gonna get a place with a —

The doors close on him mid-sentence. Still talking. Still selling.

*(On rewatch: this is the last verified human Cairns in the film.)*

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — MOMENTS LATER

The floor, quieter. Someone has already turned back to their screen.

Cairns' desk: box gone, chair still slowly spinning.

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — MORNING (PRESENT DAY)

**SUPER: ONE YEAR LATER**

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — WILL'S DESK — LATER

**THE SKILL BEAT.** A face fills the screen: a job candidate on a recorded interview. The machine's verdict: VERIFIED — 98%.

Will's finger hovers over APPROVE. Stops.

He leans in. Replays three seconds. The candidate is asked where he grew up. The eyes go — nowhere. They don't do the thing eyes do when you're actually remembering.

Will overrides: FLAG — SUSPECTED SYNTHETIC.

A pause. Then the dashboard concedes: CONFIRMED SYNTHETIC. FLAG ACCEPTED. His streak counter... resets to zero. Overrides break streaks.

Will looks around to tell someone. Eight heads, all down. It's a Tuesday.

## THE EROSION — BEAT 3: THE EDGE CASES

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — WILL'S DESK — DAY

Will's queue: THIRTY faces. All flagged "EDGE CASE" — the ones the machine is only 51% sure about. The hard ones. The leftovers.

His accuracy ticker, live in the corner: 91.2%... 90.8%... The system feeds him its hardest calls and grades him on the curve it created.

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — DAY

Will walks beside the gliding Robot Boss. Trying to explain it. Keeping pace with a monitor.

### WILL

It's only sending me the coin flips. So my number drops, and then the number is the reason for the next — you see what I'm —

Head tilt. 0.4 seconds.

### ROBOT BOSS

I hear your frustration, Will. I'll flag it upstream.

## THE EROSION — BEAT 4: SUPERVISOR

## INT. OFFICE SPACE — WILL'S DESK — DAY

An email with confetti graphics: a NEW TITLE. "Verification Supervisor." A raise so small the email calls it an "adjustment."

His new dashboard: a RIVER of the machine's decisions scrolling past, too fast to read. One button remains: INTERVENE. Fine print under it, essentially: every intervention triggers a coaching module.

Will watches the river. His cursor hovers over nothing. He REFRESHES an empty action queue. Refreshes it again. The way you refresh a phone.

The 8:55 pot steams. The lights click off in a dark department, one by one, on their timers.

**THIS is the night Cairns calls.**
