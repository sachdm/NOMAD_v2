## INT. OFFICE SPACE — DAY

Will sits at his desk. On his display: CAIRNS — poolside, sun, abundance.

### CAIRNS

You wouldn't believe how good it is here, bro. Everything's two for one. And I'm not just talking about the food — right, ladies?

*(wink wink, nudge nudge)*

### TWO GIRLS

¡Sí!

*(they laugh and giggle)*

### CAIRNS (CONT'D)

You gotta get out here, bro. It's life on crack. Same work setup — but now it comes with bottomless margaritas.

The two girls come to Cairns and do their best to distract him.

### CAIRNS (CONT'D)

Anyway, man — you know I'm tight with Michelle. Say the word and I'll get you set up down here.

The girls start actually pulling him from the screen.

### CAIRNS (CONT'D)

Ladies, ladies — calm your horses. Plenty of papi to go around. Okay? Ladies!

They really make him go now.

### CAIRNS (O.S.) (CONT'D)

Love you, buddy!!

The screen stays on. The three of them run to the pool. Cairns grabs one girl and throws her in. Will can't help but watch — a tinge of jealousy sprouting in his expression.

### JOHN (O.S.)

Is that Cairns? Nice...

Will anxiously closes the tab and turns. John, holding two beers.
